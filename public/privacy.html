Privacy notice

CKEFA Software Solutions Ltd respects the privacy of people visiting our website and contacting our team. This notice describes how we handle enquiries and website analytics information.

Who we are

CKEFA Software Solutions Ltd, United Kingdom. Contact our team at info@ckefa.co.uk for any privacy questions.

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we collect the name, email address, organisation (if provided), service category and message that you choose to share. Our hosting and anti-spam services may also process technical connection information needed to deliver and protect the site.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to questions, assess potential projects and manage relevant business communications. Depending on the circumstances, the applicable legal basis is our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Service providers

Our website is hosted using Netlify. Enquiries submitted through the website are processed by Netlify Forms and are accessible to authorised CKEFA personnel through its dashboard. We may configure an email notification to info@ckefa.co.uk. If enabled, Cloudflare Web Analytics is used to understand general website traffic and performance. See the providers’ own privacy documentation for their processing details.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business records or meet applicable obligations. Access is restricted to authorised people, and we review records periodically. No internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your rights

Subject to applicable UK data-protection rules, you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data or object to certain processing. Contact info@ckefa.co.uk. You can also raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Updates

We may update this notice when our website or data processing changes. Last updated: 20 November 2026.
